# Freelancer guide

TEST MODE only; demo account `amira@demo.workspring.test` / `demo-password-2026` (synthetic).

1. **Profile** (`/app/profile`): title, overview, skills, rate, availability → **Publish**. Your public page shows only real WorkSpring evidence: completed contracts and revealed reviews.
2. **Find work** (`/jobs`): search and filter; open a job and **Submit a proposal** (bid or hourly rate, duration, cover letter, screening answers, attachments). Track it in `/app/proposals`; withdraw if needed. Invitations from clients are in `/app/invitations`.
3. **Messages & interviews**: reply, attach files, edit/delete recent messages, block/report. Accept or decline interview proposals in `/app/interviews` and add them to your calendar (`.ics`).
4. **Offers** (`/app/offers`): read the snapshot of terms and fees, accept to start the contract.
5. **Fixed price**: when a milestone is funded, deliver with a note and files; respond to revisions; payment is released by the provider after the client approves.
6. **Hourly**: **Start timer** / **Stop timer** in the workroom (time is only tracked while the timer runs; no screenshots or activity monitoring), add manual entries if the client allowed them, then **Submit timesheet** for the week.
7. **Services** (`/app/services`): create a listing with three packages, add-ons and buyer requirements → **Submit for review** → a moderator approves or asks for changes. Manage orders in `/app/orders`: deliver, handle revisions.
8. **Agency** (`/app/agency`): create one, invite freelancers by email, assign admin/member roles, publish a public agency page.
9. **Reviews, notifications, support** as for clients.
